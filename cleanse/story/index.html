I Was a Prisoner to the Bathroom for 6 Years — Until a Pharmacist Showed Me Why

👩
By Karen S., 52 — Nashville, Tennessee
As told to TheHealthMotion ·

Diarrhea out of nowhere. Then constipated for three days. Then so bloated I'd have to lie down after breakfast just to get through the morning. Six years of this. Six years of doctors telling me it was stress, or IBS, or just how my body works now. I believed them for a long time.

I spent probably $800 on probiotics. Different brands, different strains. Cut out gluten — still bloated. Cut out dairy — still running to the bathroom. At some point I stopped telling people because I was embarrassed to still not have an answer.

My neighbor mentioned a pharmacist she'd been seeing. Dr. Lisa King. Thirty years in practice. I almost didn't go — I'd already seen four doctors. But she said something in the first ten minutes that stopped me cold: "The parasites living in your gut right now don't care how clean your diet is. And probiotics? They can't touch them."

She showed me a clinical image of what was actually happening inside my gut. I'm not going to describe it. But I finally understood why nothing had ever worked — and why the answer had nothing to do with what I was eating.

She recorded a free video explaining exactly what she found and what she did about it. I watched it that same night. I wish I'd found it six years ago.

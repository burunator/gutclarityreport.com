I Deworm My Dog Every 3 Months. It Never Once Occurred to Me to Check Myself.

👩
By Karen S., 54 — Nashville, Tennessee
As told to TheHealthMotion ·

Every few months, without fail, I give my dog his deworming treatment. I never skip it. I've done it since the day I brought him home twelve years ago. I love him. I would never let him walk around with parasites silently damaging his body. It's just what responsible pet owners do.

Last spring, I was kneeling on the kitchen floor giving him his treatment, and a thought hit me so hard I had to sit down.

I had been doing this for my dog for twelve years. I had never once done it for myself.

The Signs I Had Been Ignoring

If my dog had shown the symptoms I'd been living with, I would have rushed him to the vet immediately. But somehow, when it was my own body, I had explained everything away.

The diarrhea that came out of nowhere. The bloating so bad I'd have to unbutton my jeans after breakfast. The constipation that would last for days and then flip back overnight. The exhaustion that no amount of sleep ever fixed. I had blamed stress, getting older, something I ate.

I never once thought to ask if something was living inside me the same way it could live inside my dog.

Turns out, I wasn't being dramatic. Scientists confirm that dogs can pass gut parasites directly to the people they live with — through licking, sharing the couch, or simply stroking their fur. And unlike our pets, most of us never get checked. Most doctors never even suggest it.

What I Found

A friend mentioned a 30-year pharmacist named Dr. Lisa King who had spent years researching exactly this problem. I almost didn't look her up. I had been disappointed too many times before.

But I watched her video. And I sat at my kitchen table afterward for a long time, just thinking.

I'm not going to explain what she found — she does it far better than I could, and you need to hear it from her directly. What I will tell you is that when she walked through it, years of unexplained symptoms suddenly made complete sense. The diarrhea. The bloating. The exhaustion. The way probiotics had never touched any of it. There was a reason for all of it. And it had nothing to do with my diet, my age, or my stress levels.

These microscopic invaders compromise the gut lining, disrupt the body's ability to absorb nutrients, and produce the exact chemical signals that the brain reads as bloating, fatigue, brain fog, and unpredictable bowel movements.

Why Probiotics, Diets & Prescriptions Keep Failing

Surgeons Found a Live 3-Inch Worm in This Woman's Brain. It Had Started With Diarrhea and Bloating.

By Dr. Lisa King, PharmD ✓ 30-Year Practicing Pharmacist

A 64-year-old Australian woman spent years suffering from stomach cramps, diarrhea, and fatigue. When her symptoms grew worse — depression, forgetfulness, breathing problems — her doctors ordered an MRI. They found what appeared to be a brain tumor.

When surgeons opened her skull to remove it, they found something no physician had ever seen before in recorded medical history: a live, 3-inch roundworm, squirming inside her brain.

It had been in her body for years. Starting in her gut. Moving through her liver and lungs. Eventually reaching her brain. She had no idea.

Why This Matters For You

This isn't a rare tropical disease. Roundworms are among the most common parasites in the world. They spread through contaminated water, undercooked food, and everyday contact with infected animals — including pets. If you have a dog or cat at home, your risk is significantly higher.

In my 30 years as a pharmacist, I've seen the same pattern repeat itself. Probiotics that help for a week. Diets that change nothing. Prescriptions that make things worse. None of them were ever designed to address what's actually causing the problem.
